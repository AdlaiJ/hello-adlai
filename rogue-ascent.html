Eightfold field manual

How the entire game works

Everything you need to survive all eight realms and build a powerful run.

Movement & combat

WASD move · aim with the mouse · hold left click to fire.

SPACE or SHIFT dashes in your movement direction, or toward the mouse when standing still.

Q uses your unlocked path ability. E spends a full Soul meter on Soulburst.

Weapons

Start with the Traveler Blade. Every path unlocks four weapons at tiers 2, 4, 6, and 8.

Use the WHEEL or C to cycle everything you have unlocked.

Weapons vary in fire rate, range, spread, seeking, piercing, elements, and impact.

Floors & quests

Eight realms contain three scrolling floors each: 24 floors total.

  • Defeat the cursed or the floor guardian.
  • Awaken two altars by holding F.
  • Recover three gold relics hidden across the map.

All three quests must be complete to advance.

Leveling & skill tree

Enemy XP raises your level. Upgrade buttons remain locked for one second so an accidental click cannot choose for you.

Each of the six paths has eight tiers. Press K to inspect the complete 48-tier tree at any time.

Stats

  • Might: weapon damage
  • Vitality: health and armor
  • Agility: movement and dash rate
  • Focus: attack speed
  • Fortune: critical chance
  • Spirit: abilities and Soulburst

Press T to spend stat points.

World & survival

Walls block movement and projectiles. Mud-like terrain slows you. Golden arrows point to objectives outside the camera.

Bosses appear every third floor. Each has a unique projectile pattern and special attack.

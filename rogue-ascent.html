Eightfold field manual

How the entire game works

Everything you need to survive all eight realms and build a powerful run.

Movement & combat

WASD move · aim with the mouse · hold left click to fire.

SPACE or SHIFT dashes in your movement direction, or toward the mouse when standing still.

Q uses your unlocked path ability. E spends a full Soul meter on Soulburst.

Weapons

Start with the Traveler Blade. Every path unlocks four weapons at tiers 2, 4, 6, and 8.

Use the WHEEL or C to cycle everything you have unlocked.

Some weapons are true melee swings. Others create shockwaves or curve through enemies. Starpiercer charges while held and fires when released.

Floors & quests

Eight realms contain three scrolling floors each: 24 floors total. Every floor selects three of five possible quests.

  • Hunt a dangerous miniboss.
  • Escort a spirit safely to its gate.
  • Awaken two altars with F.
  • Recover three hidden gold relics.
  • Defend a ward until its ritual completes.

Enemy waves arrive throughout the floor and every enemy awards XP. Green healing orbs are scattered around the map.

Leveling & skill tree

Each level grants one skill point. The tree stays locked for one second so an accidental click cannot spend it.

Unlock connected circles along the branching lines. Dashed connections require another tree too. Hover a circle to reveal its effect, requirements, and universal bonuses.

Every tree has its own universal perk: lifesteal, projectile speed, cooldown recovery, dodge, improved healing, or retaliation damage. Press K to inspect the complete tree.

Stats

  • Might: weapon damage
  • Vitality: health and armor
  • Agility: movement and dash rate
  • Focus: attack speed
  • Fortune: critical chance
  • Spirit: abilities and Soulburst

Press T to spend stat points.

World & survival

Walls block movement and projectiles. Mud-like terrain slows you. Golden arrows point to objectives outside the camera.

Bosses appear every third floor. Each has a unique projectile pattern and special attack.
